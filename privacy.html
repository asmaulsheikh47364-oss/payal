Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

This policy explains, in plain words, what the Payal website (payalapp.in), the Payal Android app and the Payal Telegram bot collect, why, and where it goes.

What the website and our server keep

  • Your Gmail address, and the name and profile photo of your Google account, when you log in with Google.
  • A device ID of your phone, for the one-Gmail-one-phone rule.
  • Your plan (Free, Plus or Pro) and its dates.
  • If you are logged in to the app: your memories, recent chats and settings, saved to your Payal account so they come back on a new phone.
  • Referral records: who joined through whose invite, and any reward days that came from it.
  • Payments: the order, the payment ID, the amount and its status. Card, UPI and bank details go only to Razorpay, never to us.
  • Help messages you send us, and any payment ID or transaction (UTR) number you type in.
  • Counts of app downloads and logins, and how many minutes the app is used each day.

Where it goes

  • Supabase: our server and database, which holds the account details above.
  • Razorpay: handles payments.
  • Google: the login with Google.
  • Google Fonts and jsDelivr: the website loads its fonts and one code library from them, so they see your IP address, as with any website.

We do not sell your data to anyone.

What the app does on your phone

The app asks for each permission only when a feature needs it. You can turn any permission off in your phone settings.

  • Microphone: to hear you when you talk to Payal.
  • Notifications and SMS: read only for features you turn on, such as reading out your messages and the find-my-phone code word. OTP codes are never read aloud.
  • Location: for weather, the map, SOS and place reminders. Location in the background is used only if you allow it.
  • Contacts, calls and sending SMS: to call or message someone when you ask, to save a new contact when you ask, and for SOS and the daily check-in.
  • Call log and phone state: to tell you who is calling.
  • Camera: when you show Payal something.
  • Photos: to find or send a photo when you ask.
  • Accessibility service: to read the screen and tap or type only when you ask Payal to do a phone task, and to count time in apps if you set app time limits. It never reads password fields.
  • Screen recording: only when you ask for it.
  • App usage stats: to show your screen time.
  • List of installed apps: to open the app you ask for.
  • Showing over other apps: so Payal can appear and answer while you are using another app.
  • Do Not Disturb access: to turn silent mode or Do Not Disturb on or off when you ask.
  • Installing apps: to install Payal's own updates, and apps Payal makes for you. Your phone always asks you before installing.

Your memories and records

Memories, notes, the diary and other records are kept on your phone. If you are logged in, your memories, recent chats and settings are also saved to your Payal account on our server so they come back on a new phone. They are never sold or shared. If you choose, you can also back them up to your own Google Drive and bring them back from there. That backup is kept in your own Drive.

Conversations and the AI service

To make Payal's replies, what you say or type, and photos when you send them, is sent through your own API key to an AI service. That service handles it under its own terms. On our server, only the recent chats saved to your Payal account (when you are logged in) are kept, as explained above.

Payal Telegram bot: messages you send to the bot are processed by an AI service to write the reply. The bot keeps only the last few messages of a chat in temporary memory, not in a database. Telegram's own privacy policy also applies.

How long we keep data

Account and plan details are kept while your account exists. Payment records are kept as long as the law requires. Help messages are kept so we can follow up on your case. When you ask us to delete your account, we delete its data, except records the law requires us to keep.

Children

Payal has a kids mode, meant to be used under a parent's supervision. Payal is not meant for children to use alone. We do not sell any child's data, or anyone else's.

Your rights

You can ask us any time to delete your account and its data. Message us from the contact page and we will do it within a reasonable time (except payment records we must keep by law).

Grievance officer

For any privacy question or complaint, write to our grievance officer. We will reply as soon as we can.
