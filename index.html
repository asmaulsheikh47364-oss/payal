FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is an API key and where do I get it?

Payal builds her answers using your own Google AI Studio API key. You can create one for free at aistudio.google.com with your Google account and paste it once in the AI Brain tab. AI usage and its daily limits belong to that key.

What exactly do I get in Free?

Conversation, by voice and chat, is completely free. Camera, location, phone control and the coding partner come with Plus and Pro.

How long does a plan last? Will it auto-debit?

30 days. There is no auto-pay. When the month ends your account becomes Free again, and you can renew whenever you want.

Can I use it on two phones?

No. One Gmail runs on one phone at a time. If your phone breaks or you switch, log in with the same Gmail on the new phone. For safety the new phone activates after 7 days.

Money was debited but my plan did not open?

Wait a little: it unlocks automatically once the payment succeeds, even after a network drop. If not, enter your payment ID or UTR number and it opens right after verification. If it still does not, call us on +91 93211 09411 and we will fix it ourselves.

Are my conversations safe?

Your API key stays on your phone. Conversation text goes through your key to Google's AI service to create the reply. We do not store your conversations on our servers. We keep only your Gmail, device ID and plan status. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

Which phones does it run on?

Android 8 or newer, with internet. Some jobs (phone control) need the Accessibility permission.

Do I get refunds?

Once a digital plan is active we normally do not refund. But if you were charged twice, or charged without the plan opening and it cannot be resolved, you get a refund. See the Refund Policy.
